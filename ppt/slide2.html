OVERVIEW

Replace the paper register with a shared, cloud-backed one

What it does

  • Admins provision users, courses, and enrollments — no public signup
  • Teachers take a fast present/absent roll call on laptop or phone
  • Students check only their own attendance record
  • Course summaries and percentages for teachers and admins
  • Server-side rate limits on login, writes, and reports

The core idea: split write and read paths

Primary (Supabase Postgres) — owns every write, login lookups, and consistency-sensitive reads.

Read replica — asynchronous, read-only. Serves lists, rosters, histories, and reports. Never a failover target.

Deliberately out of scope

  • No QR / biometric / self check-in — avoids proxy check-ins
  • No offline mode, parent portal, multi-tenancy, native apps
